Nebula Bastion Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026. Developer: Đinh Mậu Trường (Dinh Mau Truong).

We do not collect your data

Nebula Bastion is a fully offline game. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or third-party tracking, and it does not send your personal information or gameplay to us or anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, Apple may include them in that backup under Apple's own terms. You can export or delete your progress in Settings.

Purchases

The optional Expanded Edition is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. The game stores on your device whether you own the edition.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: dimatru3009@gmail.com.
