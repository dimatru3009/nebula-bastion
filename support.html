I bought the Expanded Edition but it is locked

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with. You need a network connection for this step only.

I changed phones

Restore your device backup, or export your save in Settings on the old phone and import it on the new one.
